## Completed

All items from the original roadmap have been implemented:

- Line-level why (`engram why <file>:<line>`)
- Shared dashboard (`engram dashboard --serve`)
- Audit trail (`engram audit`)
- Cross-repo search (`engram search --global`)
- Interactive TUI (`engram browse`)
